How Weeks Are Read

Each entry counts for what it usually costs a night. Games, exams and travel count most, lifts and practices less, classes barely at all.

A week is called a busy one when it is heavy in itself and heavier than your own ordinary week, measured from the weeks already in your calendar.

These weights are reasoned choices, not values proven in a study. Student athletes report insufficient sleep on about four nights a week, travel moves both sleep and body clock, and exam weeks carry the highest stress in a season.
