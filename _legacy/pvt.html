Vigilance Test

This measures reaction time, which is the part of fatigue a wearable cannot see.

How It Works
  • A counter appears at random intervals, and you tap the screen the instant you see it.
  • The whole session runs for three minutes and takes no preparation.
  • Tapping before the counter appears is recorded as a false start.
  • Run it at the same time of day every time, because alertness follows a daily rhythm.
Timing Environment

Measured on this device before the test, because the result is only trustworthy if the platform noise is small.
