What Matters For Attention
Three Minutes, Validated The three-minute version of this test detects sleep loss in laboratory studies, counting any response slower than 355 milliseconds as a lapse.
Weeks, Not Single Tests A 2022 study found the short test tracks the ten-minute laboratory version only loosely, especially for lapses, so Myaku compares weeks and gives speed the larger say.
Easy To Miss When people slept six hours a night for two weeks, their reaction times kept getting worse while their sleepiness ratings levelled off.
Caffeine Hides It Caffeine speeds responses and cuts lapses during sleep loss, so a test soon after it can look better than you really are.
Same Hour Matters Alertness follows a daily rhythm, so tests taken at different hours are partly measuring the clock.
Same Phone Matters Phones differ in how fast they register a tap and draw the screen, so switching phones can shift your times.
No Practice Effect Taking the test repeatedly does not steadily improve scores, so a change across weeks is not just getting used to it.

Based on a 2011 validation study, a 2022 comparison study, a 2003 sleep restriction study, a caffeine study, a repeated testing study, and a phone latency study.
