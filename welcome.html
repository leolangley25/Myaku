Step One Of Eight

Welcome To Myaku

Most apps only look at your body. Burnout starts in more places than that.

Body. Sleep, heart rate variability, and resting heart rate from a wearable.
Brain. A three-minute reaction test, the part of fatigue a wearable cannot see.
Life. A short check-in about how your days are actually landing.

Myaku watches for the moments these three disagree, like a recovered body with a strained head. Those are the patterns a single score hides.

Setup takes about four minutes, and every step after this one can be skipped.

Who Is Using This

The three channels are the same for everyone. What changes is the calendar around them.

Myaku gives the most to someone in a full season with a team schedule, and it still works for anyone training seriously. You can change this later in About You.

Yours, Not Your Coach's

Myaku is built for you, and it stays that way.

Private By Default Nothing is shared unless you create a link, and a link shows one word, never your numbers.
Journal Stays Unread No mood is ever inferred from what you write. Only a rating you choose counts.
The Assistant Is Off Asking questions about your readings sends a summary to a model, so it stays off until you turn it on.
Leave Whenever You Want Download everything, or delete your account and every row with it, from More.
Not A Diagnosis Myaku describes patterns in your own data. It does not replace a trainer, a doctor, or a counsellor.
