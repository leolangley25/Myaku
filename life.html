What Matters For Burnout
Demand Without Control Decades of workplace research link high demands with little control over them to strain and burnout, more than demand alone.
Three Separate Signs Athlete burnout is measured as exhaustion, a reduced sense of accomplishment, and caring less about the sport, and each can move on its own.
Feeling Alone Self-determination research links unmet needs, including feeling cut off from people, with burnout in athletes.
Feelings Count What you report is a measurement too, and it covers school, sport, and life in a way no sensor can.
Not A Diagnosis This describes patterns in your own answers. A trainer, sports psychologist, or counsellor can tell you what they mean.

Based on a review of the demand and control model, the 2001 athlete burnout questionnaire, and a 2025 review of athlete burnout studies.
