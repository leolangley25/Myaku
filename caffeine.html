What Affects Sleep
Clear Timing advice from a 2023 review of 24 studies lands near this level, such as a coffee nine hours before bed. Under 30 mg
Borderline Results are mixed here. In a 2025 trial with young men, 100 mg four hours before bed made no measurable difference. 30 To 75 mg
High In the same trial, 400 mg twelve hours before bed still cut about 20 minutes of deep sleep. Over 75 mg
You May Not Notice In a 2013 study, 400 mg six hours before bed cost more than an hour of sleep, and people did not report it.
About Five Hours Half of a drink is usually gone in about five hours, but it ranges from three to seven, so every time here is an estimate.
Daily Amount Up to 400 mg a day is the common limit for healthy adults, and pediatric guidance is 100 mg for ages 12 to 18.

Based on a 2023 review, a 2025 trial, and a 2013 study. The zones are this app's estimate from those studies, not a medical cutoff.
