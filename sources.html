Wearables
Apple Health

Your iPhone can export every night of sleep and heart data it holds.

  1. Open Health, tap your profile picture, then Export All Health Data.
  2. Save the archive to a computer and unzip it.
  3. Choose the export.xml file from inside the unzipped folder.

Spreadsheet

A CSV file with a header row. Only the date column is required.

Columns can be named date, hrv, rhr, sleep_minutes, and sleep_efficiency.
