How Myaku Works

Everything behind the readings, in plain language, including what it cannot do.

Three Channels
What Normal Means

Every reading is compared against your own history, never against other people. Your normal is the middle of your recent weeks, and the spread around it is measured in a way that one strange night cannot distort.

A channel needs three earlier weeks before this week can be compared against them. That is why nothing is named during your first few weeks, even though your raw readings show from the first day.

A very consistent athlete has a small spread, and against a small spread any wobble can look dramatic. Myaku puts a floor under the spread so that being reliable is never mistaken for being unwell.

When A Pattern Appears

A channel only counts as strained when it stays above the bar for more than one week in a row, because a single bad week is what ordinary life looks like.

Sensitivity changes how far a channel has to move, and for how long, before a pattern is named. You can change it in More.

Moves are measured in spreads of your own history, so a move of one means one typical spread away from your normal. Your current setting is highlighted.

The Seven Patterns
Reading The Gaps

A gap compares two channels against each other instead of against your normal. When they agree there is nothing to report, and when they pull apart the direction of the gap says which one is ahead.

A gap carries the noise of both channels at once, so it has to move further than either channel alone before it counts.

What It Cannot Do
Not A Diagnosis Myaku describes patterns in your own data. It cannot tell you that you have burnout, depression, or anything else.
Chosen Thresholds The cutoffs above are reasoned choices, not values proven in a study, and they have not yet been checked against real outcomes.
Short Burnout Questions Each burnout question is one item from a longer published questionnaire, so it tracks your trend rather than giving a validated score.
Patterns Are Not Causes A busy week can drive two things at once without either one causing the other, so no relationship shown here proves a cause.
Devices Measure Differently Apple Watch measures heart rate variability differently from Whoop and Fitbit, so switching devices can look like a change in you.
Early Tests Improve Reaction time often gets faster over the first few sessions as you learn the task, so your first weeks can look better than they are.
Privacy

Your data is not shared with anyone unless you create a share link, and a share link shows only the name of your current pattern.

Journal text is stored and never analysed. Connection tokens for your wearable are encrypted before they are stored. You can download everything or delete your account at any time from More.
